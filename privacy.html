Rico for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Rico works on your iPhone without an account. It keeps your creative work on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

What Rico stores

Rico saves your scripts, settings, recording details, videos and audio locally on your iPhone. The app does not send this content or usage data to a Rico server. Rico has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Rico uses camera and microphone access to record video and sound. The optional Follow voice feature reacts to sound level; it does not transcribe speech. Rico requests add-only Photos access when you choose Save to Photos. Sharing happens only when you choose an option in the iOS share sheet.

Deleting and keeping copies

You can remove scripts and takes in Rico. Deleting the app removes its local data. Copies you saved to Photos, shared elsewhere, or retained in device backups must be managed there. Rico cannot delete those copies for you.

Support messages

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to answer your request. You do not need to send scripts or videos to get help. We keep support messages only as long as needed to respond and handle related follow-up; you can ask us to delete your correspondence at the address below. Cloudflare Email Routing handles incoming mail for our support domain. Your email provider and the receiving mailbox provider handle the message under their own privacy practices.

This website

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including an IP address, to deliver and secure the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site does not include analytics, advertising or contact forms.
