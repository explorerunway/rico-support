Rico support

Find your recording, finish a take and share it from your iPhone.

Contact support

Email us with a description of the issue, your iPhone model, iOS version and Rico version. Please do not send a video or script unless you choose to share it.

rico-support@vishranabuilds.com

Recording

Choose a script by tapping its title at the top of the camera. Tap Record to start and Stop to finish. To record without the reading window, open Controls and turn off Show teleprompter. Rico asks for camera and microphone permission when needed; you can check those permissions in iPhone Settings.

Finding and finishing a take

Open Takes from the camera to review recordings. A paused session can be resumed or finished later. Trimming and Polish save separate movies, so your original remains available.

Saving and sharing

Open a take, then choose Export to save a copy to Photos or share it through the iPhone share sheet. Rico requests add-only Photos permission when you choose Save to Photos. Check Photos or the destination app for copies you have exported.

Your content

Scripts and videos are stored on your iPhone. Export important videos before deleting Rico. For details, read the Privacy Policy.
